# Code of Conduct

Nubo Native Platform (NNP) follows the
[CNCF Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

As contributors and maintainers of this project, and in the interest of
fostering an open and welcoming community, we pledge to respect all people who
contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating
documentation, submitting pull requests or patches, and other activities.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the maintainers at **contribution@nubons.com**.

The full text we adhere to is maintained by the CNCF at the link above.
